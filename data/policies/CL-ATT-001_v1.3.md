# Attendance Policy
Doc-Code: CL-ATT-001
Version: 1.3
Status: approved
Category: attendance
Effective: 2026-01-01

## 1. Attendance records
Attendance for live sessions is recorded automatically by the learning platform. A learner is marked present only if they attend at least 75 percent of the session duration.

## 2. Minimum attendance
Overall attendance of at least 75 percent is required to complete the programme. At the midpoint check in week 8, a learner below 75 percent receives a written warning from their mentor.

## 3. Reporting absence
- Unplanned absence must be reported through the support portal or to the mentor within 48 hours.
- Planned leave must be reported at least 24 hours before the session.

## 4. Attendance corrections
A learner who believes a record is wrong must raise a correction request on the support portal within 5 working days of the session. The Academic coordinator verifies the request against the session recording. Only authorised staff can correct official attendance records.

## 5. Recorded sessions
Watching a recording counts as half attendance for that session. A maximum of 3 sessions per programme can be counted this way.

## 6. Assistant restrictions
The assistant must not modify attendance records or decide assessment eligibility from unverified data.
