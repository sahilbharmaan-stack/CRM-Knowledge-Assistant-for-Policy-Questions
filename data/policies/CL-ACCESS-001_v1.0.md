# Learning Accessibility and Accommodation Policy
Doc-Code: CL-ACCESS-001
Version: 1.0
Status: approved
Category: support
Effective: 2026-01-01

## 1. Requesting learning support
Learners who need an accessibility adjustment should contact Support through the approved channel, preferably before the affected class, assessment or learning activity. The request should describe the adjustment needed and the relevant activity. Learners should share only information needed to review the request and must not send passwords, payment details or unnecessary sensitive information.

## 2. Review and confirmation
The designated academic or accessibility staff member reviews each request and confirms any approved adjustment in writing. An adjustment is not active until it has been confirmed. The assistant and counsellors cannot approve adjustments or promise that a specific request will be granted.

## 3. Academic requirements
Accessibility adjustments support access to learning and do not by themselves change programme completion requirements, assessment criteria or published deadlines. Questions about assessment results or appeals follow the Assessment and Certification Policy (CL-ASSESS-001).

## 4. Assistant restrictions
The assistant may explain how to request support but must not assess a learner's needs, request medical records, approve an adjustment or state that a change has been made.
