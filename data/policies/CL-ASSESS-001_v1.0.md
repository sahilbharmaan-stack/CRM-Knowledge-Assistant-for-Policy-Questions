# Assessment and Certification Policy
Doc-Code: CL-ASSESS-001
Version: 1.0
Status: approved
Category: assessment
Effective: 2026-01-01

## 1. Assessment components
The overall score is made up of weekly quizzes (20 percent), module assignments (30 percent), the Core Project (40 percent) and the final viva or presentation (10 percent).

## 2. Pass requirement
A learner passes with an overall score of at least 60 percent and a Core Project score of at least 50 percent.

## 3. Deadlines
Assignments are due 7 days after release. Late submissions are accepted up to 48 hours after the deadline with a 10 percent penalty. After 48 hours a submission is not accepted unless the assessment owner approves it.

## 4. Resubmission
The Core Project can be resubmitted once, within 7 calendar days of the result. The score after resubmission is capped at 70 percent.

## 5. Results and appeals
Authorised evaluators publish results within 5 working days of the submission deadline. A learner can raise an appeal on the support portal within 3 working days of the result. The assessment owner reviews the appeal.

## 6. Certificates
The Academic team issues certificates within 10 working days after verifying that all completion requirements are met.

## 7. Assistant restrictions
The assistant may explain these criteria but must not grade learners, change scores or issue certificates.
