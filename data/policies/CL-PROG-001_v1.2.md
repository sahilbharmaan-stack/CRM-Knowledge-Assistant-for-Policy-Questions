# Programme Policy - Data & AI Professional Programme
Doc-Code: CL-PROG-001
Version: 1.2
Status: approved
Category: programme
Effective: 2026-01-01

## 1. Programme overview
The Data & AI Professional Programme runs for 16 weeks in hybrid mode. Each week has three live sessions of two hours each, plus self-paced labs on the learning platform.

## 2. Modules
The programme covers seven modules in this order: Python Foundations, SQL and Databases, Data Analysis, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, LLM and AI Agent Applications, and the Capstone Core Project.

## 3. Eligibility and enrolment
- Applicants must be graduates or final-year students.
- Basic computer literacy is required. Prior coding experience is not required.
- Any exception to these eligibility rules must be referred to the Programme Team. Counsellors cannot grant exceptions.

## 4. Fees and instalments
The programme fee is Rs 60,000, which includes a non-refundable registration fee of Rs 1,500. The fee can be paid in full or in three instalments. Refund rules are defined in the Refund and Withdrawal Policy (CL-REFUND-001).

## 5. Batches and start dates
New batches start every month. The Programme Team confirms the exact start date at least 3 days before the batch begins.

## 6. Completion requirements
To complete the programme a learner must meet all three conditions: attendance of at least 75 percent (see CL-ATT-001), an overall assessment score of at least 60 percent (see CL-ASSESS-001), and submission of the Core Project.

## 7. Counsellor restrictions
Counsellors must not promise admission, placement, certification, salary outcomes or any particular result. Statements must be supported by this policy or another approved document.
