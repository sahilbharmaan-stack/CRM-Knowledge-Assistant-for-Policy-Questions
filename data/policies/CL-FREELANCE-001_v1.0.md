# Progression to Freelancing Projects
Doc-Code: CL-FREELANCE-001
Version: 1.0
Status: approved
Category: freelancing
Effective: 2026-01-01

## 1. Eligibility to proceed
Successful completion of the assigned Core Project and qualification under the evaluation criteria may make a participant eligible for the next stage of the freelancing project process.

## 2. Conditions
- The participant must complete and submit the assigned Core Project within 7 calendar days of allocation.
- The project must be evaluated and must qualify under the applicable criteria.
- The participant must meet the applicable programme requirements.
- A suitable freelancing project must be available.

## 3. No automatic allocation
Freelancing project allocation is not automatic or guaranteed because a Core Project has been submitted. Submission alone does not establish qualification or entitlement.

## 4. Blockers
If a genuine blocker may affect the deadline, the participant must inform the Support/Project Team before the deadline and explain the issue.

## 5. Assistant restrictions
The assistant must not promise a freelancing project, guarantee allocation or predict qualification. It must not change evaluation status. If a participant's result or current project availability is not in an approved source, the assistant must say it cannot verify this and refer them to the programme team.
