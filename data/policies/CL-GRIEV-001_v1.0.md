# Learner Concern and Complaint Resolution Policy
Doc-Code: CL-GRIEV-001
Version: 1.0
Status: approved
Category: support
Effective: 2026-01-01

## 1. Raising a concern
Learners may raise a concern or complaint through the approved Support channel. Include a short description, the programme or course, relevant dates and a non-sensitive reference number when available. Do not include passwords, verification codes, payment-card details or unnecessary personal information.

## 2. Review and escalation
Support routes the concern to the appropriate team and follows the response targets in the Student Support Policy (CL-SUPPORT-001). If an issue remains unresolved, the learner may ask Support to escalate it. Support escalates unresolved issues to the team lead and then to the Programme Manager as described in CL-SUPPORT-001.

## 3. Specialist decisions
Refund disputes are reviewed by Finance under the Refund and Withdrawal Policy (CL-REFUND-001). Assessment result appeals follow the process and deadlines in the Assessment and Certification Policy (CL-ASSESS-001). A complaint does not itself change a refund, assessment result or enrolment record.

## 4. Assistant restrictions
The assistant may explain the reporting and escalation route. It must not claim that a complaint was filed, promise an outcome or make a decision reserved for Support, Finance, the Programme Team or an authorised evaluator.
