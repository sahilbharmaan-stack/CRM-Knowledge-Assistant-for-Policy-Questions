# Batch Transfer and Deferral Policy
Doc-Code: CL-TRANSFER-001
Version: 1.0
Status: approved
Category: programme
Effective: 2026-01-01

## 1. Requesting a change
An enrolled learner may request a batch transfer or programme deferral through the approved Support channel. Requests should include the current batch, the requested batch or deferral period, and a brief reason. Learners should submit requests before the current batch starts whenever possible.

## 2. Review and availability
The Programme Team reviews each request and confirms whether a change is possible. A transfer depends on an available place and programme requirements; a requested change is not approved until the learner receives written confirmation.

## 3. Requests after a programme starts
Requests made after the programme has started are reviewed case by case by the Programme Team. The assistant and counsellors cannot approve a transfer, reserve a place or change enrolment records.

## 4. Fees and withdrawal
Any fee or refund question related to a transfer, deferral or withdrawal must be handled under the approved Refund and Withdrawal Policy (CL-REFUND-001). This policy does not promise a fee adjustment or refund.

## 5. Assistant restrictions
The assistant may explain the request and review process but must not claim a request was submitted or approved, or change a learner's enrolment.
