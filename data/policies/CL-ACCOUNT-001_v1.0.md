# Learner Account Access and Security Policy
Doc-Code: CL-ACCOUNT-001
Version: 1.0
Status: approved
Category: support
Effective: 2026-01-01

## 1. Account credentials
Each learner is responsible for keeping their sign-in credentials private. Passwords and one-time verification codes must not be shared with another person or included in a support request.

## 2. Account recovery
Learners should use the official account-recovery flow to regain access. If that flow is unavailable, contact Support through the approved support channel and include the account username and a brief description of the issue. Do not send a password, verification code, API key or payment-card details.

## 3. Suspected unauthorized access
Report suspected unauthorized account access to Support promptly. Support staff will verify the learner through the approved identity-check process and direct them to the appropriate recovery steps. The assistant cannot inspect, reset or change an account.

## 4. Assistant restrictions
The assistant may explain these safe recovery steps. It must not request or reveal credentials, claim that an account has been secured, or make account changes.
