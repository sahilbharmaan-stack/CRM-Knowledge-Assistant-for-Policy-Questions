# Refund and Withdrawal Policy
Doc-Code: CL-REFUND-001
Version: 2.1
Status: approved
Category: refund
Effective: 2026-01-01

## 1. Scope
This policy applies to learners who withdraw from the Data & AI Professional Programme and request a refund of fees paid.

## 2. Refund tiers
A refund tier applies only when both its time condition and its attendance condition are met. Time is counted in calendar days from the enrolment date.
- Tier A: request made within 7 days of enrolment and attendance below 10 percent of scheduled sessions. Refund is 100 percent of fees paid, minus the non-refundable registration fee of Rs 1,500 and payment gateway charges.
- Tier B: request made between day 8 and day 21 of enrolment and attendance of 25 percent or less. Refund is 50 percent of the programme fee paid, excluding the registration fee.
- Tier C: request made after day 21, or attendance above 25 percent. No refund is payable.

## 3. Request process
1. The learner submits the Refund/Withdrawal Request form on the support portal.
2. The Finance team verifies enrolment date, payment record and attendance.
3. Finance records a decision and reason within 5 working days.
4. If approved, the refund is processed to the original payment method within 10 working days of approval.

## 4. Instalments
When a withdrawal is approved, unpaid instalments are cancelled. Instalments already paid follow the refund tiers in section 2.

## 5. Exceptions
Medical emergencies or documented hardship may be reviewed by the Finance Head. Counsellors and support staff cannot grant exceptions. Disputed or unclear cases must be escalated to the Finance team.

## 6. Assistant and counsellor restrictions
The assistant may explain this policy and suggest which tier may apply. It must not approve, reject or initiate a refund, and it must not promise a refund amount. Final decisions are made only by the Finance team.
