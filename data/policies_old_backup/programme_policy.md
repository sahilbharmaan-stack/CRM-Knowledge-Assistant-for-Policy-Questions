# Programme and Certificate Policy
Doc-Code: PRG-001
Version: 1.2
Status: approved

## Programme duration
The standard programme duration is 12 weeks, including a final capstone project.

## Certificate eligibility
A certificate is issued after at least 75 percent attendance, successful capstone submission, and full fee payment. Certificates are issued only by the Academic team.

## Batch transfer
A learner may transfer to a later batch once, free of charge, if requested at least 5 working days before the batch start date.
