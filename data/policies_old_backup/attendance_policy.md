# Attendance Policy
Doc-Code: ATT-001
Version: 1.4
Status: approved

## Minimum attendance
Learners must attend at least 75 percent of live sessions to remain eligible for the programme certificate.

## Missed sessions
If a learner misses a live session, the session recording is available within 24 hours. Learners may request one make-up session per module.

## Leave of absence
A learner may apply for a leave of absence of up to 14 days for medical or emergency reasons. Supporting documents must be submitted to the counsellor within 3 working days of returning.

## Late joining
Joining more than 15 minutes after the session start time is recorded as late. Three late arrivals count as one absence.
