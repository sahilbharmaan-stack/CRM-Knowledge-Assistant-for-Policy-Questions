# Refund and Cancellation Policy
Doc-Code: REF-001
Version: 2.1
Status: approved

## Refund eligibility
A learner may request a full refund within 7 calendar days of enrolment, provided less than 10 percent of the programme content has been accessed.

## Partial refund
After 7 days and up to 14 days from enrolment, a partial refund of 50 percent of the programme fee may be considered. A processing fee of 5 percent is deducted from any refund.

## No refund
No refund is available after 14 days from enrolment, or once a certificate has been issued, or if the learner has completed more than 25 percent of the programme.

## Refund processing time
Approved refunds are credited to the original payment method within 10 working days. Refund approval is done only by the Finance team after counsellor submission.
