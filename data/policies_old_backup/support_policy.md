# Learner Support Policy
Doc-Code: SUP-001
Version: 1.0
Status: approved

## Response time
Support tickets receive a first response within 24 working hours. Urgent access issues such as login failure are answered within 4 working hours.

## Escalation
If a ticket is not resolved within 3 working days, the counsellor may escalate it to the Support Lead. Complaints about conduct are escalated directly to the Programme Manager.

## Support channels
Learners can contact support through the helpdesk portal or the support email. Phone support is available on weekdays from 10 AM to 6 PM IST.
